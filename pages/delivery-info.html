⚠️ Important Delivery Information

Please ensure someone is available to receive your delivery. If no one is available, the courier will attempt redelivery or leave the package in a safe location. Failed deliveries may incur additional charges.

Collection Information

Pick up your order from our affiliated cafe partners or Royal Mail collection points. Here's what you need to know:

  • Affiliated Cafes: Multiple locations across London with flexible pickup times
  • Royal Mail Points: Over 11,500 collection points nationwide
  • Identification: Please bring a form of ID and your order confirmation
  • Notification: You'll receive a text/email when your order is ready for collection
  • Collection Code: Use your unique collection code at the pickup location
  • Storage: Orders can be stored for up to 18 days after delivery to collection point
